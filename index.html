About Me

Get to know me better

I am Bhuwan Paneru, a Computer Engineer and Cybersecurity Analyst with an academic background from the National Academy of Science and Technology (NAST), affiliated with Pokhara University. My foundation in computer science, networking, and software development has shaped my interest in cybersecurity and the protection of digital infrastructure. I am particularly interested in Security Operations Center (SOC) operations, threat detection, security monitoring, incident response, and threat intelligence. Through hands-on labs and continuous learning, I am developing practical skills in SIEM tools, log analysis, security event investigation, vulnerability assessment, and defensive security practices. My approach to cybersecurity emphasizes analytical thinking, continuous improvement, and staying updated with emerging threats and security technologies. I aim to contribute to identifying security risks, mitigating cyber threats, and helping organizations build stronger security postures. I believe that cybersecurity is not just about defending systems but also about creating a safer digital environment for everyone. My goal is to continue growing as a cybersecurity professional while applying my knowledge and skills to solve real-world security challenges.
